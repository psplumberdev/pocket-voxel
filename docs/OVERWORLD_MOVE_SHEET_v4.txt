POKEMON RED / BLUE - OVERWORLD BATTLE MOVE IMPLEMENTATION SHEET
=================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
This is a CODING DESIGN SHEET for the optional real-time "Overworld Battles" mode.
The 165 move names are the Generation I move roster. MELEE/RANGED/SELF classification
below is OUR action-game implementation choice, not a canonical Pokemon category.


LATEST OVERWORLD SIMPLIFICATION RULES
-------------------------------------
- Normal move cooldown: 3 seconds.
- Multi-hit moves: 1 second cooldown; intentionally button-mash friendly.
- Flinch: immobilize target for 2 seconds.
- Trap moves (Bind, Wrap, Fire Spin, Clamp): immobilize target for 10 seconds on hit.
- Whirlwind and Roar: push target back 2 tiles.
- Two-turn/charge moves: one charge-up period, then attack; no turn system.
- Quick Attack / priority-style moves: fast dash/lunge.
- Teleport: temporary ranged damage move with 15-tile range.
- Bide, Counter, Mirror Move, Substitute: use Tackle behavior for now and leave TODO notes.
- Rage: Tackle-style melee plus temporary +5 stacking rule, maximum 5 stacks.
- Thrash and Petal Dance: use ordinary attack behavior for now; retain TODO notes for special repeat/lock behavior.
- Self-only buffs/status effects last 10 seconds; reapplying refreshes the timer.
- Stat stages use -6 to +6.

MELEE HIT AREA
--------------
Melee hits the 5 adjacent front-facing squares wrapped around the Pokemon:
front-left, front, front-right, left-side, right-side. Rear 3 adjacent squares are excluded.

CORE DELIVERY RULES
-------------------
MELEE:
- Hits the five adjacent front-facing tiles wrapped around the Pokemon:
  forward-left, forward, forward-right, left-side, right-side.
- One tile deep only.
- Rear three adjacent tiles are excluded.

RANGED:
- Fires/places a simple effect in the facing direction.
- Prototype visual may remain a single shaded square.
- Normal projectile speed = roughly bicycle movement speed.
- Status/debuff moves that target an opponent are RANGED unless explicitly noted AREA.

SELF:
- Applies directly to the user; no projectile required.

AREA:
- Ranged/AoE behavior centered on user or target as noted.

GENERAL ACTION-MODE RULES
-------------------------
- Normal attack cooldown: 3.0 seconds unless a future move-specific rule overrides it.
- Stat stages are clamped from -6 to +6.
- SELF stat buffs/status buffs last 10 seconds.
- Reapplying a SELF buff refreshes its 10-second timer and applies the move's stage change,
  up to the +6 cap.
- Damage/stat math should reuse the existing battle backend wherever possible.
- Poison/Burn/Sleep/Paralysis/Confusion/Freeze/Trap/DOT are TARGET status systems and should
  be implemented as action-mode status effects rather than discarded.
- Multi-hit attacks should use the existing hit-count/damage behavior; do not turn each hit
  into a separate 3-second cooldown.
- Special two-turn moves can be stubbed initially but are explicitly marked for later behavior.
